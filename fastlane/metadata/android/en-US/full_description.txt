<p><i>DistroHopper</i> is a home screen replacement for Android that can resemble a number of different Linux desktops, including Ubuntu’s Unity desktop, elementary OS’s Pantheon desktop and the Gnome desktop.</p>
<p>Features include a couple of different themes, a search feature which allows you to search from a multitude of different search sources (both local and remote), and customisation options.</p>
<p><b>Optional swipe-to-open-notifications feature:</b> DistroHopper lets you open your notification shade with a swipe gesture on the home screen. This feature is completely optional — it's turned off by default, and you never have to use it. If you choose to enable this feature, DistroHopper uses an Android Accessibility Service to perform this one action for you, because it's the only way a home screen app like DistroHopper can open the notification shade. When enabled, the service does just this and nothing more: it does not read, collect, or share anything on your screen, and it doesn't watch what you do. You enable it yourself in your device's Accessibility settings, and you can turn it back off there at any time.</p>
